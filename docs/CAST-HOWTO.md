# Keep your characters looking the same: a quick guide

Recurring characters need references, or every picture is a fresh guess and your hero looks like a
different person from shot to shot. The **Cast** panel on your video's page fixes that. Describe each
character once, add a picture, lock their look with a character sheet, then tick who is in each shot.
The studio adds the right pictures and description to every shot with that character. Things and
settings that must look the same every time (a red umbrella, the harbour cabin) work the same way:
see [Props and places](#7-props-and-places).

Technical details are in [CAST.md](CAST.md) and [PROPS.md](PROPS.md).

## 1. Add your characters

- **New video from an idea:** on the review step, the **Characters** card lists everyone the draft
  wrote. Check each name, the part they play and what they look like. Add a picture if you have one
  and answer **Whose pictures are these?** (see step 2). They join the cast when you start.
- **On the video's page:** open **Cast**, then **Add someone to the cast**: a name, the part they
  play, and what they look like.

Write the look the way you want it on screen, every time: age, build, face, hair, colours, clothing.
That exact description goes into every shot the character is in.

## 2. Add a reference picture

Under the character, choose a picture and answer **Whose picture is this?**:

- **I made it myself (my own original work)**, or **I own the rights to use it**.
- Tick **It shows a real person** only with that person's permission, then confirm **That person
  agreed to their likeness being used in this video**. Without that, real people are refused.

Then press **Upload picture**. What works:

- PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 15 MB (your studio may set other limits). Small pictures are fine: from
  128 pixels on each side. Under 512 pixels you see "Small image, we'll sharpen it up" and the studio
  sharpens it up for the models; your file stays as it is.
- A clear, full-body picture on a plain background works best. The first picture becomes the
  **Main picture**; **Make main** changes it.
- Your pictures are private: only the people working on this video (and admins) can see them. See
  [Who can see and change a cast](#who-can-see-and-change-a-cast).

## 3. Make and lock a character sheet

A character sheet is a set of matching pictures of one look: a main full-body picture, the turnaround
(front, three-quarter, side and back) and a head close-up sheet. Once you lock it, every shot with the
character uses those pictures.

1. **Pick a starting point.** Under **Make the options from**, choose **My main picture** (it stays the
   reference for the face and design) or **The description only**. Choose **How many options**.
2. **Check the price.** Press **Check the price**. Sheet pictures are paid and charged to this video's
   budget, so the price shows before anything is made. In preview mode they are free stand-ins, so
   you can try every step at no cost. Press **Make the options**.
3. **Pick a look.** When the options appear, press **Use this look** on the one you like. None of
   them right? **None of these: make new options**.
4. **Make the turnaround.** Press **Check the price of the turnaround**, then **Make the turnaround**.
   Not happy? **Redo the turnaround**, or **Start the sheet over**.
5. **Approve and lock.** When every picture looks like the same character, press **Approve and lock
   this look**. The sheet can't be changed after this, and the description it was locked with stays
   with it.

**Admins only:** the words of a locked look (its description and outfit) can still be changed, also
during a run, with **Change the locked words** under the look. Give the reason. The pictures stay.

Paid sheets need the video in live mode (**Enable Live Mode** on the video's page). Before each
picture, the studio checks the price, your balance and this video's budget again. If one of them
doesn't fit, that picture isn't made and the panel says why.

## 4. Add an outfit

A locked character can have more outfits. Under the locked look, use **New outfit**: give it a name
(for example *Winter coat*) and describe the clothes, then **Add the outfit**. Make and lock its
sheet the same way. Outfits are always made from the locked look, never from scene pictures, so the
face and body stay the same.

## 5. Choose who is in each shot

Under **Who is in each shot**, tap the names of everyone in the picture, then **Save who is in each
shot**.

- When a character has more than one outfit, choose it under **[Name] wears**. **Their usual look**
  is the first look you locked. Locking a new outfit doesn't change shots that already use the usual
  look.
- Pictures have no words in them by default: no captions, speech bubbles or lettering. For a shot
  that needs words (a sign or a title card), tick **Words may appear in this picture**.
- While the video is being made, who is in each shot can't change. A sheet locked after the video
  started is used the next time you make it.

## 6. Reuse a cast in later videos

Press **Start a series with this cast**. In your next video, pick the series under **Series
(optional)** on the review step, or press **Use this series** on the video's page. The cast and its
locked looks come along. Changes to a series cast show up in every video of the series. **Stop using
the series cast** gives this video its own empty cast again. The series keeps everyone.

## 7. Props and places

Open **Props & places** on your video's page, just below the cast.

1. Under **What is it?**, choose **A prop** (a thing in the shot, like a red umbrella) or **A place**
   (where the shot happens, like a harbour cabin).
2. Choose a photo, give it a name, and describe it under **What it looks like**. Describe it the way
   it should look in every shot: shape, size, colours, materials, or for a place the room, light and
   what is always there.
3. Answer **Whose picture is this?**, as for a cast picture.
4. Press **Add with this photo**. No photo yet? **Add without a photo** works too: the studio uses
   the description until you add one.

More about photos:

- **Add another photo** adds another angle or a close-up. **Use as main** picks the photo the studio
  uses first.
- Photos follow the rules for cast pictures: PNG, JPG or WebP, at least 128 pixels on each side, up
  to 15 MB. Real people need their permission.
- Photos are private (see [Who can see and change a cast](#who-can-see-and-change-a-cast)).
- In a series, every video of the series shares its props and places.
- While the video is being made, props, places and their photos can't be removed. Wait for the run
  to end.

## 8. Pick them on each shot, and fix a prompt

Under **Shot plan**, you can change each shot before the video starts, and again while it waits for
**Approve the shot plan**:

- **Pick what is in each shot.** Tap the shot's **Place** (one), its **Props** (up to 4) and its
  **Cast**. Tap again to remove a pick.
- **Fix a prompt.** **Edit the prompt** changes what the scene picture shows, and the end picture and
  motion prompts when the shot has them.
- **Save.** Changed shots say **Not saved yet**. Add a reason under **Why the change (optional)** if
  you like, then press **Save 1 shot** (or **Save 2 shots**, and so on). **Approve the shot plan**
  waits until you save or undo.
- **Check the result.** While the video waits for your approval, the studio checks the plan again
  (model limits and prices) before you approve it. Each saved shot then lists **Made with:** the
  photos it will be made with. **History** shows who changed what, with the before and after.

The studio sends the picked photos with the shot's scene picture and adds your descriptions to its
prompt. The clip starts from that picture and is told to keep them as they look. Shots you pick
nothing for are made just as before.

Once you approve the shot plan, it stays as it is for this video's run. Only the video's owner (or an
admin) can change it.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "each side must be at least 128 px" | Use a bigger picture. |
| "Small image, we'll sharpen it up" | Nothing: the picture is kept. A bigger one looks sharper. |
| "not a readable PNG, JPG or WebP image" | The file is damaged or another type. Export it again as PNG or JPG. |
| "That file is too large" | Save a smaller copy (under 15 MB). |
| "Confirm the image is yours to use" | Answer **Whose picture is this?** before uploading. |
| "switch it to live mode first" | Sheet pictures are paid. Press **Enable Live Mode** on the video's page. |
| "Some pictures were not made" | The list says why (for example, the price, your balance or the budget changed). Check the price again and make them again. |
| "is being made right now" | Wait for the current run to end, then change the cast. |
| "Version 1 is locked" | Locked looks can't change. Add a **New outfit** instead, or ask an admin to change its words. |
| "Only the people working on this episode (and admins) can see its cast" | The video belongs to someone else. Ask its owner or an admin. |
| "is a place, not a prop" (or "a prop, not a place") | Pick it under **Place** instead, or under **Props**. |
| "needs a picture prompt" | A shot's picture prompt can't be empty. Write one, or **Undo**. |
| "Save or undo your shot changes first." | Press **Save** (or **Undo**) under the shots, then approve. |
| "Saved …, but the plan breaks a model limit" | Fix what the message names (for example, shorten the prompt), save again, then approve. |
| "The shot plan … cannot change now" | The shot plan is approved for this run. Change it after the run ends, for the next one. |

## Who can see and change a cast

- **Seeing:** a video's cast is seen by the video's owner and admins. A series cast is seen by the
  series' owner, the owners of the videos in the series, and admins. A video without an owner (an
  older, shared one) can be seen and changed by everyone in the studio, and so can its cast. Anyone
  else gets "not found", even for a picture's link, and their series list leaves out the series they
  can't see.
- **Changing:** only the video's owner (or an admin) can change its cast, and only a series' owner (or
  an admin) can change a series cast. A series without an owner (an older one) can be changed only by
  an admin.
- **Props and places** follow the same rules as the cast, photos included. A video's shot plan is
  seen by whoever can see its cast and changed by the video's owner (or an admin).
