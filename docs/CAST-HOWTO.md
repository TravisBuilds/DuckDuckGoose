# Keep your characters looking the same: a quick guide

Recurring characters need references, or every picture is a fresh guess and your hero looks like a
different person from shot to shot. The **Cast** panel on your video's page fixes that. Describe each
character once, add a picture, lock their look with a character sheet, then tick who is in each shot.
The studio adds the right pictures and description to every shot with that character.

Technical details are in [CAST.md](CAST.md).

## 1. Add your characters

- **New video from an idea:** on the review step, the **Characters** card lists everyone the draft
  wrote. Check each name, the part they play and what they look like. Add a picture if you have one
  and answer **Whose pictures are these?** (see step 2). They join the cast when you start.
- **On the video's page:** open **Cast**, then **Add someone to the cast**: a name, the part they
  play, and what they look like.

Write the look the way you want it on screen, every time: age, build, face, hair, colours, clothing.
That exact description goes into every shot the character is in.

## 2. Add a reference picture

Under the character, choose a picture and answer **Whose picture is this?**:

- **I made it myself (my own original work)**, or **I own the rights to use it**.
- Tick **It shows a real person** only with that person's permission, then confirm **That person
  agreed to their likeness being used in this video**. Without that, real people are refused.

Then press **Upload picture**. What works:

- PNG, JPG or WebP, at least 512 pixels on each side, up to 15 MB (your studio may set other limits).
- A clear, full-body picture on a plain background works best. The first picture becomes the
  **Main picture**; **Make main** changes it.
- Your pictures are private: only signed-in members of your studio can see them.

## 3. Make and lock a character sheet

A character sheet is a set of matching pictures of one look: a main full-body picture, the turnaround
(front, three-quarter, side and back) and a head close-up sheet. Once you lock it, every shot with the
character uses those pictures.

1. **Pick a starting point.** Under **Make the options from**, choose **My main picture** (it stays the
   reference for the face and design) or **The description only**. Choose **How many options**.
2. **Check the price.** Press **Check the price**. Sheet pictures are paid and charged to this video's
   budget, so the price shows before anything is made. In preview mode they are free stand-ins, so
   you can try every step at no cost. Press **Make the options**.
3. **Pick a look.** When the options appear, press **Use this look** on the one you like. None of
   them right? **None of these: make new options**.
4. **Make the turnaround.** Press **Check the price of the turnaround**, then **Make the turnaround**.
   Not happy? **Redo the turnaround**, or **Start the sheet over**.
5. **Approve and lock.** When every picture looks like the same character, press **Approve and lock
   this look**. The sheet can't be changed after this, and the description it was locked with stays
   with it.

Paid sheets need the video in live mode (**Enable Live Mode** on the video's page). Before each
picture, the studio checks the price, your balance and this video's budget again. If one of them
doesn't fit, that picture isn't made and the panel says why.

## 4. Add an outfit

A locked character can have more outfits. Under the locked look, use **New outfit**: give it a name
(for example *Winter coat*) and describe the clothes, then **Add the outfit**. Make and lock its
sheet the same way. Outfits are always made from the locked look, never from scene pictures, so the
face and body stay the same.

## 5. Choose who is in each shot

Under **Who is in each shot**, tap the names of everyone in the picture, then **Save who is in each
shot**.

- When a character has more than one outfit, choose it under **[Name] wears**. **Their usual look**
  is the first look you locked. Locking a new outfit doesn't change shots that already use the usual
  look.
- Pictures have no words in them by default: no captions, speech bubbles or lettering. For a shot
  that needs words (a sign or a title card), tick **Words may appear in this picture**.
- While the video is being made, who is in each shot can't change. A sheet locked after the video
  started is used the next time you make it.

## 6. Reuse a cast in later videos

Press **Start a series with this cast**. In your next video, pick the series under **Series
(optional)** on the review step, or press **Use this series** on the video's page. The cast and its
locked looks come along. Changes to a series cast show up in every video of the series. **Stop using
the series cast** gives this video its own empty cast again. The series keeps everyone.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "each side must be at least 512 px" | Use a bigger picture. |
| "not a readable PNG, JPG or WebP image" | The file is damaged or another type. Export it again as PNG or JPG. |
| "That file is too large" | Save a smaller copy (under 15 MB). |
| "Confirm the image is yours to use" | Answer **Whose picture is this?** before uploading. |
| "switch it to live mode first" | Sheet pictures are paid. Press **Enable Live Mode** on the video's page. |
| "Some pictures were not made" | The list says why (for example, the price, your balance or the budget changed). Check the price again and make them again. |
| "is being made right now" | Wait for the current run to end, then change the cast. |
| "Version 1 is locked" | Locked looks can't change. Add a **New outfit** instead. |

Only the video's owner (or an admin) can change its cast, and only a series' owner (or an admin) can
change a series cast. Everyone else in the studio can view it.
